Personal Loan EMI Calculator

Personal loans are unsecured credit products designed for short-term needs�medical bills, weddings, small home repairs or debt consolidation. Interest rates are generally higher than secured loans because there is no collateral. Typical rates in India range from 11% to 24% depending on borrower profile and lender. Use this page to estimate EMI, compare tenures and understand eligibility.

Eligibility & key features

Eligibility typically requires stable income, minimum CIBIL score (often 650+), and proof of employment or business income. Personal loans have tenures between 1�5 years, quick disbursal timelines, and may include processing fees and prepayment clauses. Lenders evaluate DTI/FOIR before offering the final amount.

FAQs � Personal Loans

  1. Q: Can personal loans be prepaid? A: Yes, but many lenders charge prepayment or foreclosure fees�confirm before applying.
  2. Q: How does tenure affect EMI? A: Longer tenure lowers EMI but increases total interest; choose based on monthly budget and overall cost.
  3. Q: Is documentation heavy? A: No�personal loans have minimal documentation compared to home loans.
  4. Q: Can I get a better rate by adding a co-applicant? A: Sometimes yes�adding a salaried co-applicant with good credit can improve eligibility or rate.
  5. Q: Should I use a personal loan for balance transfer? A: Evaluate transfer fees vs savings; the calculator helps compute breakeven.
