Car Loan EMI Calculator

Calculate EMIs for car loans and compare the impact of down payment, tenure, and interest rates. Whether you're budgeting for a hatchback or an SUV, this tool helps you plan financing.

How EMI Calculation Works

Car loan EMIs are calculated using a standard formula. The key inputs are the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure you choose.

EMI Formula

The standard calculation is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

P = Principal loan amount (₹)
r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
n = Total months (tenure years × 12)

Example: ₹7,00,000 at 9% for 5 years gives an EMI of around ₹14,500.

Use the Calculator

Enter the loan amount, expected interest rate, and tenure to see your monthly EMI and total interest paid over the loan term.

Why Down Payment Matters

A higher down payment reduces the loan amount and lowers your EMI. Most lenders require 10–20% down payment for car loans.

Tips to Lower Your Car Loan EMI

  • Choose a shorter tenure if your budget allows to reduce interest paid.
  • Improve your credit score to get better interest rates.
  • Negotiate loan terms and compare multiple offers before finalizing.
  • Consider prepayment if the lender permits it without heavy penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal car loan tenure?

Most car loans are between 3 and 7 years. Shorter tenures cost less in interest but raise monthly EMI.

Can I prepay my car loan?

Many lenders allow prepayment, but check if there are any charges.

Does this calculator store my info?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and no personal data is stored.
