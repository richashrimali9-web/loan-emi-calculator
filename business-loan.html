Business Loan EMI Calculator

Plan business loan repayments and understand how interest impacts your cash flow. Use the calculator for loans used for working capital, inventory, or equipment.

How EMI Calculation Works

Business loans use the same EMI formula as personal loans. You can adjust tenure and rate assumptions to model different cash flow scenarios.

EMI Formula

EMI is calculated as:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

P = Principal loan amount (₹)
r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
n = Total months (tenure years × 12)

Example: ₹20,00,000 at 11% for 5 years gives an EMI of around ₹43,000.

Common Business Loan Uses

  • Working capital for daily operations
  • Purchasing equipment or machinery
  • Expanding to a new location
  • Stock or inventory financing

Tips for Business Loan EMIs

  • Match loan tenure to the asset life or cash flow cycle.
  • Maintain good financials to negotiate lower interest rates.
  • Protect cash flow by avoiding overly high EMIs.
  • Review prepayment terms to see if early repayment is beneficial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best tenure for a business loan?

A good tenure aligns with your cash flow and the useful life of the asset being financed. Shorter tenures save interest, longer tenures lower EMIs.

Can I prepay a business loan?

Many lenders allow prepayment; check for any penalties in your loan agreement.

Is this calculator accurate for all loans?

Yes, it uses the standard EMI formula. Your lender may charge additional fees that affect your effective cost.
