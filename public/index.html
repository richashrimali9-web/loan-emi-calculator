Why EMI Planning Matters

An EMI is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan. The amount depends on the principal, annual interest rate, and repayment tenure, so even a small change in any one of those inputs can change your total cost significantly.

Our calculator is designed to help Indian borrowers compare scenarios before speaking to a lender. It is especially useful for exploring longer tenures, lower down payments, and rate assumptions.

What to Verify Before You Apply

Before signing a loan agreement, compare key borrowing terms to avoid hidden fees:

✓

Rates & Prepayment Fees

Compare annual interest rate, processing charges, and foreclosure terms.

✓

Fixed vs Floating Benchmark

Ask whether your rate tracks RBI repo resets and how often terms adjust.

✓

Budget & Emergency Savings

Maintain at least 6 months of EMI reserve before taking long-term debt.
