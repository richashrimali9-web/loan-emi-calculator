Frequently Asked Support Questions

1. How accurate are the EMI calculations on mytotalemi?

Our calculators use the exact reducing-balance EMI mathematical formula mandated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Minor differences in actual bank quotes can occur due to rounding rules, GST on processing fees, or daily balance compounding versus monthly compounding.

2. Can mytotalemi help me get a loan or negotiate interest rates?

No. mytotalemi is a 100% independent financial calculation engine and educational publisher. We do not sell loans, act as credit brokers, or collect borrower contact details for lead generation.

3. How do I report an issue or suggest a new calculator feature?

You can fill out the contact form below or send an email directly to support@mytotalemi.co.in. Our technical team will acknowledge your report promptly.
