About mytotalemi

Loan EMI Calculator is a free online tool designed to help Indian borrowers understand and calculate loan EMIs. We provide accurate calculations and educational content to support informed financial decisions.

Our Mission

We believe financial literacy should be accessible to everyone. Our mission is to:

  • Provide accurate, transparent EMI calculations for all major loan types
  • Educate borrowers about loans, interest rates, and financial planning
  • Help people save thousands of rupees by making informed borrowing decisions
  • Make complex financial concepts simple and understandable
  • Build a community of financially aware borrowers

What We Offer

  • Home Loan EMI Calculator - Calculate with different down payments and tenures
  • Car Loan EMI Calculator - Compare vehicle loan scenarios
  • Personal Loan EMI Calculator - Get instant personal loan EMI
  • Business Loan EMI Calculator - Plan your business financing
  • Education Loan EMI Calculator - Calculate education loan EMI with grace period

💡 Expert Insights

  • Updates on government schemes and subsidies
  • Guides on refinancing, loan restructuring, and pre-payment strategies

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loan EMI Calculator affiliated with any bank?

No, we are completely independent. We don't represent any bank or financial institution. Our goal is to provide unbiased information and tools.

Are the EMI calculations accurate?

Yes, we use the standard declining balance method used by all banks in India. Our calculations match bank calculators exactly.

Will you save my personal information?

No. We don't ask for or store any personal information. All calculations happen on your device and are not saved anywhere.

Is there any charge to use the calculator?

No, all our calculators and guides are completely free. No registration required, no hidden charges.

Can I use the calculator on my mobile phone?

Yes, our platform is fully mobile-responsive and works seamlessly on all devices.
