Blog

Practical notes on EMI planning, loan comparisons, and borrower questions in India.

How to read this blog

The articles here are written as an educational resource for EMI planning, repayment trade-offs, and common loan questions. They use practical examples and plain-language guidance rather than prescriptive financial advice.

New to EMI? Start with the basics of EMI, monthly cash flow, and repayment structure. Choosing between loans? Compare home, personal, and car loans before you speak to a lender. Looking to reduce cost? Read about prepayment, refinancing, and repayment trade-offs.

Editorial note: These articles are written for educational purposes. Examples are illustrative, and lender terms, fees, and eligibility rules can change over time.
