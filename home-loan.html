How EMI Calculation Works

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed monthly payment that includes both interest and principal. In the early months, the interest portion is higher; later, the principal portion grows.

EMI formula

The formula used by banks in India is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

P = Principal loan amount (₹)
r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
n = Total months (tenure years × 12)

Example: ₹50,00,000 at 8.5% for 20 years gives an EMI of ~₹42,780.

Choosing a Loan Tenure

Longer tenure lowers EMI but increases total interest. Shorter tenure increases EMI but reduces overall cost. Use the calculator to compare tenure scenarios side by side.

Longer Tenure

  • Lower EMI eases monthly budget
  • Higher total interest cost
  • Good for low initial income

Shorter Tenure

  • Higher EMI but lower total interest
  • Loan completes faster
  • Best if cash flow is stable

Tips to Lower Your EMI

  • Increase down payment to reduce loan principal and EMI.
  • Keep your credit score high to access better rates.
  • Negotiate rate cuts after the first year or when rates fall.
  • Consider prepayment (check lender's prepayment policy).
  • Compare offers with our blog and guides.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I reduce EMI by increasing down payment?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the principal, which lowers EMI and total interest.

2. How does prepayment change my loan?

Prepayments reduce the outstanding principal and can reduce EMI or reduce the loan tenure. Check your lender's prepayment charges.

3. Is floating rate better than fixed rate?

Floating may start lower but can rise; fixed gives stability. Use the calculator with different rate assumptions to compare.

4. What is the minimum tenure for home loans?

Most banks offer tenures from 5 to 30 years. Shorter tenures mean higher EMI but less interest.

5. Can I transfer my home loan to another bank?

Yes, this is called a home loan balance transfer. Compare savings after transfer charges before switching.

6. What documents are needed for home loan approval?

You typically need ID proof, address proof, income proof, bank statements, and property papers. Specific requirements vary by lender.

7. How do I claim tax benefits on a home loan?

You can claim deductions under Section 80C (principal) and Section 24(b) (interest). Read our Home Loan EMI Guide for details.

8. Is it safe to use this calculator?

Yes. We do not collect or store personal info. Calculations run in your browser and are not shared.
