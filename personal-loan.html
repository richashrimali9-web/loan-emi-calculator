Use our personal loan EMI calculator to compare EMIs for different loan amounts, interest rates, and tenures. This helps you plan expenses like weddings, medical bills, or education.

How EMI Calculation Works

Personal loans are usually unsecured and have higher interest rates than secured loans. The EMI formula is the same, but a higher rate means a higher monthly installment.

EMI Formula

EMI is calculated as:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

P = Principal loan amount (₹)
r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
n = Total months (tenure years × 12)

Example: ₹3,00,000 at 12% for 3 years gives an EMI of around ₹10,000.

Common Uses for Personal Loans

  • Weddings and celebrations
  • Medical emergencies
  • Home improvement projects
  • Education or skill development

Tips to Lower Your EMI

  • Choose a shorter tenure if you can afford a slightly higher EMI, to pay less interest overall.
  • Improve your credit score to qualify for better interest rates.
  • Limit your loan amount to what you really need; avoid borrowing more than necessary.
  • Check prepayment rules so you can save interest when you have extra funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a personal loan?

Many lenders offer quick approvals, often within a few hours to a day, especially for existing customers.

Can I prepay a personal loan?

Yes, but check if your lender charges prepayment fees.
