Education Loan Options and Repayment Strategies

Last Updated: February 28, 2026 | Verified against RBI Guidelines & Current Bank Policies

From application to repayment: understand moratoriums, subsidies, co-borrowers, and strategies to minimize total interest on student loans.

Types of education loans

  • Domestic study loans: For recognized Indian institutions; lower amounts and simpler documentation.
  • Overseas study loans: Higher ticket sizes; often need collateral or co-borrower with strong income.
  • Government-supported schemes: Interest subsidy for eligible categories; check current notifications.
  • Skills/short courses: Smaller amounts, faster processing.

Moratorium, interest accrual, and capitalization

Most education loans include a moratorium (course period plus 6–12 months). Interest usually accrues during moratorium and may be capitalized (added to principal) at repayment start, increasing EMIs. If affordable, pay at least simple interest during moratorium to keep principal low.

Loan: ?25,00,000 at 12.5% p.a. Tenure: 10 years (120 months). 24-month moratorium with interest accrual.

  • EMI after capitalization ˜ ?36,000–?38,000 (rounded)
  • Prepaying 5% principal annually after employment can cut total interest by several lakhs.

Repayment strategies

  • Pay simple interest during moratorium: Prevents ballooning principal and keeps EMIs manageable.
  • Choose tenure carefully: Start with a comfortable EMI, then prepay when income rises.
  • Refinance: After securing a stable job, consider refinancing to a lower rate.
  • Automate payments: Avoid missed EMI charges; build a 2–3 month EMI buffer.

Guidance for parents/co-borrowers

  • Plan for down payment or collateral if required.
  • Review insurance options that cover the borrower during the study period.
  • Maintain healthy credit utilization to support the student's eligibility.

FAQs

Is there a subsidy for economically weaker sections?

Some government schemes offer interest subsidy during moratorium for eligible categories. Check current guidelines.

Can I prepay education loans without charges?

Varies by lender/product; many allow part-prepayment without penalty. Confirm terms in your sanction letter.

Do I need collateral?

High-ticket overseas loans often need collateral or a strong co-borrower. Domestic loans for lower amounts may be unsecured.
