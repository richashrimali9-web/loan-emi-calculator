About mytotalemi

mytotalemi helps Indian borrowers understand common loan questions in plain language. The site combines an EMI calculator, practical guides, and comparison articles so readers can compare scenarios before they speak to a lender.

What this site does

We explain how EMI works, how tenure and rate changes affect monthly payments, and how common loan decisions can influence total cost. The content is written for educational use and is meant to support informed planning rather than replace lender-specific advice.

Last updated: July 2026

Who it is for

  • Borrowers comparing home, car, personal, or education loans
  • Readers trying to understand EMI, interest, and repayment trade-offs
  • People looking for plain-language explanations before applying or refinancing

How we prepare content

  • We use standard financial formulas and explain the inputs clearly.
  • We describe assumptions and note where lender terms can differ.
  • We aim to be transparent about fees, rounding, and other factors that can change a final quote.

What this site does not do

mytotalemi is not a lender, bank, broker, or legal adviser. We do not approve loans, issue offers, or provide personalised financial advice for a specific borrower. For an official quote, please contact the lender directly and review the full agreement before acting.

Editorial note

Content on this site is prepared for educational purposes. Rates, fees, eligibility criteria, and policy terms can change by lender and over time, so the examples here should be treated as illustrative rather than final.
