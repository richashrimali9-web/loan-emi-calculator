Phase 1

Standard RBI Formulas

We strictly adhere to reducing-balance interest math as prescribed by Reserve Bank of India guidelines. Every input parameter is clearly disclosed.

Phase 2

Independent Transparency

We are not owned by, affiliated with, or funded by any commercial bank or NBFC. Our comparisons are 100% unbiased and objective.
