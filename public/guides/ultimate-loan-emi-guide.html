EMI Formula & Amortization (Practical)

Key insight

Early EMIs are interest-heavy. Prepayments in the first years yield the largest interest reduction. Evaluate both reducing EMI and reducing tenure when making prepayments ” reducing tenure saves more interest.

How to Choose: Home Loan vs Personal Loan

Home loans are secured, cheaper, and offer tax benefits; personal loans are unsecured, faster, and usually much costlier. Choose based on purpose, urgency, tenure needed, and tax considerations.

Quick decision rules

  • If buying/constructing property ? prefer home loan (lower rate, tax benefits).
  • If urgent short-term need and speed > cost ? personal loan may be acceptable.
  • For renovations/business where collateral exists ? Loan Against Property often beats personal loan.

Loan Comparison Checklist (All-in cost)

Compare offers using these dimensions ” don't rely on headline rate alone.

  • Nominal rate and rate type (fixed vs floating)
  • All-in cost (APR-like): processing + insurance + other one-time fees
  • Prepayment/foreclosure rules: charges, allowed timings, part-payment minimums
  • Processing, valuation, legal fees and whether they are refundable
  • Turnaround time for disbursal and documentation friction
  • Eligibility constraints that may change your actual offered rate (DTI/FOIR, credit score)

Prepayment & Refinancing ” Short Guide

Prepayment reduces outstanding principal; refinancing (balance transfer) moves loan to a cheaper lender. Always compute NPV of savings vs switching costs (processing, valuation, legal).
