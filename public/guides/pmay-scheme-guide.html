How the Subsidy Reduces Your EMI

Example 1: EWS borrower, Bangalore

How to Apply

  1. Verify eligibility on PMAY portal
  2. Gather documents: KYC, income proof, property details, self-declaration
  3. Apply for home loan and PMAY subsidy simultaneously at participating bank
  4. Bank uploads to PMAY MIS portal; authority verifies and sanctions subsidy
  5. After disbursal, subsidy credited to loan account, EMI recalculated

Regional & State Variations

Some states add top-up benefits or higher area caps:

  • Maharashtra: Extra ₹15,000 grant for women borrowers
  • Kerala: Higher carpet area caps for MIG categories (132 sqm)
  • Karnataka: Fast-track processing for Bengaluru project

Always confirm with state housing board or bank branch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underreporting income (risk loan cancellation)
  • Property area overstatement (cap violation)
  • Owning any pucca house previously
  • Using non-notified area property
  • Choosing a non-participating bank
  • Missing 6-month window after disbursal

FAQs

Can I claim PMAY for resale property?

Yes if property meets area and income criteria and loan is for first purchase under PMAY.

Is PMAY available for home improvement?

Some categories permit improvements; check latest circulars or bank notices.

How long does subsidy credit take?

Typically 30–60 days post disbursal but may take longer due to administrative delays.
