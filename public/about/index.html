About mytotalemi

mytotalemi is a free educational website for Indian borrowers. We publish EMI calculators, plain-language loan guides, and comparison content to help people understand borrowing costs before they apply for a loan.

Editorial approach

We explain the assumptions behind each calculation and keep the content focused on practical decision-making. Our guides are updated regularly and are intended to support informed financial planning rather than replace professional advice.

Last updated: July 2026

What we offer

  • EMI calculators for home, car, personal, and education loans
  • Step-by-step explainers for how EMI works and what changes total interest
  • Loan comparison articles, common mistake guides, and planning resources

How we present information

  • We use standard financial formulas and explain the inputs clearly.
  • We note when figures are estimates and when lender terms may differ.
  • We aim to be transparent about fees, assumptions, and limitations.

Trust and disclosure

mytotalemi is not a lender and does not provide loan approval or disbursement services. Our calculators are educational tools and may not reflect the exact terms offered by a bank or financial institution.

If you need an official loan offer, please contact the lender directly and review the full agreement before applying.
