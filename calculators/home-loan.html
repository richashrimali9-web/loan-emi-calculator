Frequently Asked Questions

Is prepayment allowed on home loans in India?

Yes, per RBI guidelines, individual floating-rate home loans carry zero prepayment penalty.

What is the ideal tenure for a home loan?

A 15-to-20 year tenure balances affordable monthly EMIs with controlled total interest paid over time.

How does down payment affect total cost?

Higher down payments reduce the principal borrowed, directly lowering total interest outgo.
