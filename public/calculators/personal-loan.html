Frequently Asked Questions

Do personal loans require collateral?

No, personal loans are unsecured loans issued based on your credit score and monthly income.

Can I prepay a personal loan early?

Yes, though some lenders charge 2% to 5% foreclosure fees on pre-closing personal loans.
