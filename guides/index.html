Loan & EMI Guides

Practical guides for EMI basics, loan comparisons, repayment choices, and common borrower questions in India.

How to use these guides

These guides are meant to help you understand EMI calculations, loan trade-offs, and common borrower questions before you speak to a lender. They explain assumptions and practical scenarios in plain language and are best used as a starting point for your own comparison.

Understanding EMI Learn what EMI means in practice and how monthly payments relate to total cost. Doing the math See how principal, tenure, and rate affect EMI and total interest. Planning repayment Review common repayment choices such as prepayment and refinancing.

Editorial note: These guides are prepared for educational use. Loan terms, fees, reset rules, and eligibility criteria can change by lender and over time, so the examples here should be treated as illustrative.
